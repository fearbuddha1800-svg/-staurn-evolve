# Hermes Quant HQ

An owner-operated, single-account demo trading research and monitoring system. It unifies Telegram ingestion, deterministic risk controls, MT5 execution, protective management, PineTS research, neural evaluation and the LuxAlgo ecosystem.

## Product truth and boundaries
- Owner uses desktop and phone; prioritize account/position safety, source traceability and honest availability.
- Dashboard and Pine research are read-only for broker orders. No browser execution buttons, no unprotected entries, no automatic risk increases.
- Existing strategies, config and 2×ATR/no-channel-TP policy remain unchanged during UI recovery.
- One application, focused views: Overview, Chart Lab, Signals, Strategies, Intelligence, System. Every installed integration is discoverable without a wall of panels.
- The requested visual direction is dark, precise, high-tech mission control with restrained cyan accents and ample spacing; not fictional AGI claims.
- Missing, stale, insufficient and disconnected are valid visible states, never replaced with fabricated data.
- Single-tenant local deployment is not a commercial multi-tenant service. No profitability or universal Pine compatibility claims.

## Development provider
Current recovery work uses GPT-6.1-sol via the user's authenticated ChatGPT/openai-codex subscription. No new API keys, charges, public deployments or broker accounts are authorized.
