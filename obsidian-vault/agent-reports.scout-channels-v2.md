# Scout Report v2 — Reputable Gold (XAUUSD) + Forex Text-Signal Telegram Channels

**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Author:** Scout agent (re-run; v1 report was lost before delivery)
**Scope:** Telegram channels posting clear TEXT signals (entry/SL/TP before the move) for gold (XAUUSD) and forex, with independent verification, loss transparency, and no funnel/VIP-upsell pattern.
**Method constraints this session:** web_search only (web_extract backend broken), no Telegram account access, no code execution. All claims below are sourced from search-result content of the cited pages; links were **not** live-fetched. Re-open each verification link before subscribing — this niche changes fast.

## 1. Executive Summary

- No gold/forex text-signal channel found has everything (auto-verified live account + proven vs-random edge + clean text signals + no funnel). The realistic shortlist is "most verifiable, least scammy," not "proven profitable."
- Independent measurement is brutal: **TrackedSignals found 147 of 196 channels (≥50 signals) lost money for followers** after spread/costs; **Verpips found only 6 of 17 gold channels in the green** (median −0.04R/trade) while channels announce "TP hit" **52× more often than "SL hit"**; measured stop-out rate is 36%.
- Several formerly-verified names have decayed or gone bad since past surveys: **1000pip Builder pivoted to education and its site went dark**; **Elite Forex Trades sits under an active FCA unauthorised-firm warning (Dec 2025, updated May 2026)**; **ApexBull's free feed was graded E** by signaltrack.io (25.4% win rate, 90% false result claims).
- Top 3 by independent-verification strength: **XAUHQ** (live auto-tracked Myfxbook accounts), **SureShotFX** (5,234-signal TrackedSignals record + trade-by-trade link audit of its loss-inclusive recaps), **Gold Signals VIP @gold_forex_signals_vip** (signaltrack.io grade C, positive expectancy, 0% false result claims).
- For the signal-relay use case: only channels 1–5 in the shortlist post consistently parseable text signals (side/entry/SL/TP). Vasily-style "analysis" channels without a stop are unusable for automated relay.

## 2. Vetting Criteria Applied

A channel had to pass (or be explicitly forgiven for) each of these, in order of weight:

1. **Text signals before the move.** Side + entry + stop + target posted before price gets there. No SL = automatic fail (Verpips: 55 of 100 signals read across Telegram lacked stops; a no-stop post is unmeasurable and unrelayable).
2. **Independent verification — ranked by strength:**
   - **(a) Auto-verified live account:** Myfxbook "track record verified / trading privileges verified" or FX Blue verified (broker-synced; user cannot manipulate). Strongest form.
   - **(b) Third-party replay measurement:** TrackedSignals.com (replays every signal at the price a follower gets 1 min after posting, with spread/commission, vs random entries; tracks deleted posts), signaltrack.io (replays on candles, scores false result claims/edits/deletions, A–E trust grades), verpips.com (tick-by-tick gold study), ChainRated (mostly crypto).
   - **(c) Auditable self-reporting:** per-trade recaps that deep-link to the original signal messages, losses included — checkable by anyone.
   - **(d) Screenshots / "verified" badges with no named verifier** = worthless.
3. **Losses posted with the same prominence as wins.** Recaps must name losing trades and link them to the original calls. (Benchmark: United Kings posted 506 win announcements vs 4 stop-loss announcements in 60 days — arithmetically impossible at its advertised 85%.)
4. **R:R sanity.** Win-rate claim must beat the break-even rate its own stops/targets require (median channel measured by Verpips needs 55% just to break even; median actual win rate 51%).
5. **No deletion/editing.** TrackedSignals publishes deleted-post shares (XAUHQ 15%, SMC FOREX SIGNELS 37%, Gold Club House 51%). Verpips: 6 of 100 signals get edited after posting.
6. **No funnel/VIP-upsell/regulator-flagged pattern.** Regulators (FCA's finfluencer enforcement; the Elite Forex Trades FCA warning is a live example in exactly this niche) warn about: free channel as ad window → DM-to-join-VIP → broker referral codes → account-management offers (234 of 571 channels Verpips read offer to manage your money) → crypto "loss recovery" schemes. Heavy funnel ≠ instant fail, but caps the verdict.
7. **Currently active, verifiable identity.** Named operator, ≥3 years operating, service not pivoted to education or dead. (This is what killed several past "verified" names — see §5.)
8. **Healthy skepticism toward listicles.** "Best signals 2026" articles are usually paid placements (TrackedSignals says this outright). Two listicle darlings below failed on inspection.

## 3. Shortlist (ranked; 8 channels + 1 bonus)

- **Verification (strongest form found):** live **auto-tracked Myfxbook member profile** [myfxbook.com/members/GoldSignalsmyfx] — accounts: *XAUHQ* Real MT5 +536.25% / **66.30% drawdown**; *FGS 10k Live* Real MT5 +100.26% / 55.12% DD; plus a $50k **demo** client account. Myfxbook reviews page 4.8/5, 76 reviews, claimed by provider [myfxbook.com/reviews/signal-providers/forexgoldsignalcom]. Also independently scored by TrackedSignals: **321 signals measured (Feb 2024–Sep 2026), 15% of posts deleted** [trackedsignals.com/channels/gold].
- **Text-signal readability:** good — structured posts with entry zones, SL, TP1–TP4; weekly pip-by-pip performance reports; posts both winning and losing trades (channel-content survey via Telagon: telagon.io/c/@goldsignals).

- **Verdict:** *Strongest live-account verification of any gold channel found; aggressive risk profile and post-deletion mean monitor the free feed and size down before trusting it.*

## 6. Market Reality Check (why the shortlist looks thin)

- TrackedSignals (method page): a channel only "beats random entries" if fewer than **1 in 2,000** random-entry runs do as well, both history halves are positive, and it beats random stop/target placement. **147 of 196** channels with ≥50 measured signals lost followers money after costs.
- Verpips gold study (Sep 2024–Oct 2026, 3,975 signals, 17 channels): **6 of 17 profitable**; median −0.04R/trade; channels announce TP-hit **52×** more than SL-hit while true stop-out rate is 36%; only **4 of 571** channels in its directory link any verifiable account; 268 of 571 sell a VIP and 234 offer account management.
- Practical relay constraints (Verpips): 4 of 50 channels lose measurable money at just **30 seconds' copy delay**; 41 of 50 tolerate 5 minutes. For an MT5 relay pipeline, prefer channels with limit-order entries and stops ≥ structure width, and timestamp the parse.
- Regulator context: the FCA's finfluencer/signal-group enforcement pattern (free channel → VIP upsell → broker kickbacks → account management) is exactly what the §2 criteria filter for; Elite Forex Trades is the live example in this niche.
