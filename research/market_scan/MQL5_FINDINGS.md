## Official rules and validation boundaries

- **V — Market rules:** formal automatic publication tests and historical Strategy Tester checks do not establish future results or error-free operation. Paid EA demos execute trading only in the tester; indicator demos use visual testing. Seller identity approval is not strategy certification. [Market rules, I.9–10, II.1, V.1–8](https://www.mql5.com/en/market/rules).
- **V — Seller constraints:** rules restrict external licensing systems, personal-data collection, near-duplicate products and off-site support links. These are platform requirements, not a finding that any sampled product complies or violates them. [Market rules, IV.6–10](https://www.mql5.com/en/market/rules).
- **V — Validation engineering:** MetaQuotes describes funds/volume checks, order and symbol-volume limits, stop/freeze distances, insufficient history, runtime faults and resource consumption. These are useful acceptance-test dimensions, not evidence of strategy edge. [Official validation article](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/2555).
- **V — External AI constraint:** WebRequest requires allowed URLs, blocks synchronously, is available to EAs/scripts rather than indicators, and cannot run in Strategy Tester. **Inference:** a conventional tester result cannot by itself validate an active cloud AI path implemented through WebRequest; investigate stubs, recorded responses or disabled AI. Product transport implementations remain U. [Official WebRequest reference](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/network/webrequest).

## Product implications and validation questions

1. Trade panels converge on visible pre-entry risk, editable chart levels, partial exits and saved state. A useful differentiator to validate is explicit fee treatment plus safe behavior after partial fills, rejected modifications and terminal restart. CM explicitly excludes commission from its displayed estimate.
2. Scanners compress symbols × timeframes into one workspace. Validate closed-bar versus forming-bar semantics, data readiness, stale-cell indication and alert deduplication. RSI Kernel documents confirmation delay; that disclosure is more actionable than an unexplained accuracy label.
3. Copier setup is an operational workflow: role, scope, symbol mapping and sizing need inspection before activation. Validate netting/hedging compatibility, duplicate prevention, disconnect recovery and rejection reporting; do not assume source and receiver fills match.
4. AI is not one product category: the sample includes unspecified adaptive logic, claimed embedded neural models and named cloud providers. Require model/version identification, data-flow disclosure, deterministic fallback, API budget and outage behavior before accepting an AI claim as an engineering capability.
5. Gold-specific packaging simplifies onboarding but does not establish portability. Validate symbol suffixes, tick value/size, lot steps, spread filters and broker execution assumptions with controlled non-live tests in a separately authorized evaluation.

## Evidence limits

- This is a purposive sample, with multiple gold/AI products and no seller-market-share census. Search discoverability and recent listings bias coverage. Do not infer category prevalence or demand from row counts.
- Public page metadata can change; web-tool responses expose crawl dates and may be cached. The access date records this research retrieval. Search and opened-page metadata differed for Unlocked AI (price/version), so volatile prices, ratings and sales counts are deliberately omitted.
- Excluded: [AI Gold Trader](https://www.mql5.com/en/market/product/197362) appeared in search but direct opening redirected to the Experts category; availability could not be confirmed. No browser fallback was needed for the included pages.
- Reviews, seller backtests, scarcity language, sales counts, prices and linked signals cannot rank profitability. Signals were not audited; no model provenance, out-of-sample record, execution parity or actual safety behavior was established.
- Only public metadata and short original summaries are retained. No paid code or long listing text is reproduced. This report makes no purchase recommendation.
